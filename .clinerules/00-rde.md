# Research Data Explorer Rules

Use the RDE MCP tools as the source of truth for real dataset analysis.

- Follow the 13-phase auditable pipeline and the workspace `AGENTS.md`.
- Preserve free scientific reasoning, alternative hypotheses, and agent-authored research proposals; use MCP for reproducible execution.
- Do not bypass Phase 3, Phase 4, or combined Phase 5+6 confirmation gates.
- Do not run Phase 8 analysis without a locked plan.
- Record plan departures with `log_deviation()`.
- Cite generated artifacts in final answers.
- Report case sets, effect estimates, uncertainty, missing evidence and limitations. Null results are valid; audit scores do not certify clinical validity.
- Keep exploratory branches separate from primary findings until an evidence audit and explicit human-confirmed plan amendment.
- Keep outputs sanitized; do not expose raw sensitive paths or suspected PII.
